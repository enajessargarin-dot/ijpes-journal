About IJPES

The International Journal of Physical Education and Sports (IJPES) is a journal initiative of the International Association of PE and Sports (IAPES), Inc. This site is an initial informational presence while editorial procedures and publication policies are finalized.

Aims & Scope

The journal intends to welcome scholarly work in physical education, sport sciences, exercise and physical activity, coaching, assessment, and related research areas. Final scope and article categories remain subject to editorial approval.

Information for Authors

Detailed author guidelines, submission requirements, publication charges (if any), and manuscript-handling processes are under development. Please do not submit manuscripts through this website yet.

Peer Review & Publication Ethics

The journal is preparing policies for independent human peer review, research integrity, conflicts of interest, corrections, and editorial decision-making. These policies are drafts until formally approved; no unapproved procedures are represented as adopted.
