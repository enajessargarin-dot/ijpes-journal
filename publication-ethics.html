Peer Review & Publication Ethics

Policy development notice: The journal's detailed peer-review and publication ethics policies are being drafted for editorial approval. The descriptions on this page are planning information, not adopted journal rules.

Proposed peer-review framework

The journal intends to use independent human reviewers with relevant subject expertise, supported by editorial screening, conflict-of-interest checks, and documented editorial decisions. The review model, timelines, appeal process, and reviewer instructions will be published after approval.

Research and publication integrity

Policies being prepared address originality, plagiarism, authorship, research ethics, consent and privacy, competing interests, corrections, retractions, complaints, and responsible disclosure of AI assistance.

Editorial accountability

Editorial decisions will remain the responsibility of authorized human editors. No indexing, ISSN registration, or DOI registration is claimed on this website.
