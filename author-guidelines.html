Information for Authors

Manuscript submissions are not yet open. The information below describes the intended scope and preparatory requirements, not finalized submission instructions.

Research areas under consideration

Physical education; sport and exercise sciences; physical activity and health; coaching and sport performance; measurement, assessment, and educational technology; and related interdisciplinary research.

Before submitting

Prospective authors should prepare an original manuscript, accurate author and affiliation information, appropriate ethics documentation when applicable, and transparent declarations of funding, competing interests, and any AI-assisted work. Detailed formats, article categories, fees, and submission procedures remain subject to editorial approval.

Submission status

Do not send manuscripts until the journal formally announces that submissions are open. For general questions, contact ijpes.editorial@gmail.com.
